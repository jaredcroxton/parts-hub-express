# Codex review: colour schemes (gpt-6-astra, 2026-09-14)

PICK: 1 Blueprint Sheet

RANKING: 1st: 1 Blueprint Sheet, the most cohesive engineering palette and strongest secondary-text readability. 2nd: 3 Quarry, distinctive warm neutrals with credible industrial character. 3rd: 2 Graphite, professional but its greyer canvas feels flatter. 4th: 4 Original B, crisp black ink but less deliberate neutrals and four orange contrast failures.

WHY THE PICK WINS:
- Cool paper, near-black navy and orange make the drawing-set aesthetic feel intentional and premium.
- The ink reads as authoritative without becoming harsh. It suits technical purchasing and large trade orders.
- Main text achieves 16.32:1 against the canvas; secondary text achieves 9.57:1. That provides useful contrast headroom for outdoor reading.
- Orange feels like a precise engineering annotation. Its restrained use gives stock messaging and schematic markers clear emphasis.
- The supplied report records 19 passes and no failures.

RISKS WITH THE PICK:
- The navy is visibly blue in large areas, especially the footer. It needs to be accepted as the brand’s near-black.
- Accent against ground passes at approximately 3.13:1, leaving limited headroom for difficult viewing conditions.
- Pale hairlines provide subtle organisation but may disappear outdoors. Essential control boundaries must not depend on those decorative tokens alone.
- Desktop screenshots cannot establish actual phone readability in sunlight.

ONE CHANGE THAT WOULD IMPROVE THE PICK: Change accent from #f0501f to #e94b1b. It preserves the burnt-orange character while raising contrast against ground from 3.13:1 to 3.35:1. Keep accentText at #c63a00.

IF THE CLIENT INSISTS ON OPTION 4 ORIGINAL B: Change only accent from #ff5b29 to #f45120 and accentText from #ff5b29 to #c63a00. Accent then achieves 3.33:1 on canvas and 3.12:1 on ground; accentText achieves 5.02:1 on canvas and 5.24:1 on white. These two changes resolve all four reported failures while preserving the black ink and existing neutrals.

CONFIDENCE: high
