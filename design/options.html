What is shared by all three

The flow is locked. The three directions are skins on one page skeleton. You are choosing how the site looks and feels, not how it works.

  1. Same page skeleton: utility bar, header with search as the dominant control, Brand / Machine model / Part category selector, hero, parts to suit, browse by category, featured shelf of eight real SKUs, the difference, trade account and quote, the finder repeated, operational footer.
  2. Search-first entry. The search field is the largest control in the header, 44 to 48px tall, locked into its button with no gap.
  3. Three dependent selects with counts on every row. Locked selects say why. No Year step, because the export has no year data.
  4. Part number is the first line of every tile, set in mono exactly as Odoo holds it, with OEM cross-references in brackets where the name carries one.
  5. Monochrome base with one saturated accent that has a written job. Structure drawn with 1px rules, not tinted boxes. Tiles are cells in a grid, not cards.
  6. Radius 0 to 4px. No blur shadows. Depth by surface inversion only. Dark is a band, never the page theme.
  7. Thumbnails never scale up. Every photo sits at native pixels on a uniform plate with a 1px keyline. No blend modes.
  8. The no-photo state is the same plate with the part number in mono, the sub-category beneath and "Photo to come" at the bottom edge. No camera icon, no grey box.
  9. Prices in AUD, GST named on every price, inc GST by default with the toggle for trade. Quote-only parts show "Quote" and one "Request quote" action.
  10. Stock as a small square swatch plus a mono label with a real quantity. Stock never borrows the accent colour. No invented warehouse location.
  11. Copy voice: numbers first, nouns second, no adjectives, sentence case. Fitment uses "suit" and "fits", never "genuine" or "authorised". Ten OEM makes as text wordmarks with counts and a non-affiliation line, never a logo wall.
  12. Anti-slop rules: no indigo or violet, no emoji icons, no accent stripes, no cards by default, no serif or italic swaps, no cream and terracotta, no navy plus safety orange, no quarry hero, no big-stat hero, no gradients, no glass, no pill filter rows, no lorem.

Prices shown in all three options are placeholders until the Odoo sync is live. Photos are the current 128px Odoo thumbnails; larger client photography drops into the same plates later.
